Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Shorts Bot ("the app") is a private tool used only by its owner to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's YouTube channels.

What data the app accesses

With the owner's permission, granted through Google sign-in, the app uses two Google OAuth scopes:

  • youtube.upload: to upload videos, with their titles and descriptions, to the owner's channel.
  • youtube.readonly: to read the connected channel's name, ID and subscriber count, which confirms the right channel is connected.

The app does not access viewers' data, comments, email, contacts, or any other Google service.

How data is stored and used

OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a file that only the owner's user account can read. They are used only to upload the owner's videos. No data is sold or shared, and none is used for advertising, analytics, or training AI models. The app has no servers and no third-party data processors.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or through Google security settings. The tokens stored on the owner's computer are deleted when the owner disconnects the app.

Contact

For questions, contact the owner through the "About" page of the Trading FX or History Talk YouTube channel.
